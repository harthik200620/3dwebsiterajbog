Questions, answered straight

How much gosht, really?

We count it before we seal it. Every meat handi lists its pieces and weight on the menu card. If it is not there, dinner is on us.

How spicy is it?

Aromatic first, layered second, fiery only if you ask. Pick your heat at checkout, from raja mild to angaara.

Veg and halal?

All meat is halal. The veg menu is a full dum menu of its own, cooked in separate handis, never shared.

How long to my hall?

Dum finishes to order, so we quote forty minutes to campus gates and we mean it. The seal keeps it steaming to your door.
